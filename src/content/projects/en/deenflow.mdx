# DeenFlow – Pomodoro Productivity Platform

**DeenFlow** is a productivity platform designed to help users stay focused and manage their time effectively using the Pomodoro technique. We developed their website, incorporating unique features to create an engaging and personalized user experience. You can explore it at [deenflow.com](https://deenflow.com).

## Key Project Features

- **Customizable Timer**: Users can set the duration of their work sessions and breaks according to their personal preferences, allowing for a more flexible and effective workflow.  
- **Detailed Statistics**: The platform tracks completed sessions, total focused time, and progress over time, helping users visualize their productivity and stay motivated.  
- **Discord Integration**: We developed a Discord bot that enables users to start, monitor, and manage their Pomodoro sessions directly from Discord, fostering community interaction and accountability.  
- **Responsive Design**: The website is optimized for all devices, ensuring a seamless user experience on both desktop and mobile.  
- **Light and Dark Mode**: Users can switch between light and dark themes to suit their preferences and enhance comfort during different times of the day.

The final product is an intuitive, functional platform that supports users in improving their time management and boosting productivity through the effective use of the Pomodoro technique.
